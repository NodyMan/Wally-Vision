Slide through infrared bands, open a spectrum or a light curve in one click,

=== WHY THESE WORDS ===
- Epsilon Indi B is the field whose motion was measured (6.0 ± 1.1″ vs 6.5″). WISE 0855 is not used: no shift is
  measurable for it in SPHEREx (peak 2–4x the noise, next to a background star).
- "candidates to check", never "discoveries" (COMMON §13).
- No Gaia/CatWISE line: the catalogue layer needs IRSA TAP, which timed out all of 15 Nov, so it cannot be shown.
- Spectrum and light curve are labelled "preview" in the app (3x3-pixel photometry on single exposures).
